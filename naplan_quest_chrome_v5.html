📲 Add to your Home Screen

On iPad (any browser):

  1. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) in the toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  3. Tap Add. A "Numeracy Quest" icon appears like any app — full screen, no browser bars.

On Mac (Chrome):

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Click the ⋮ menu (top right) ▸ Save and Share ▸ Create Shortcut...
  3. Tick "Open as window", then click Create. An icon appears in Launchpad/Applications and opens as its own app window, no browser bars.

On Mac (Safari 17+):

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Click File ▸ Add to Dock (or the Share icon ▸ Add to Dock).
  3. It now opens as its own app window from the Dock.

Everything runs locally in the browser — no internet needed after the first load, and your medals are saved on this device.
